My MSc thesis investigated the automated segmentation of sperm cells and their anatomical components from microscopic images using deep learning. The work developed an image-processing and deep-learning pipeline to identify and segment the head, acrosome, nucleus, and tail of individual sperm cells. The approach combines image enhancement, convolutional neural networks, pixel-level classification, and morphological processing to produce accurate and reliable segmentation maps.

The resulting framework enables automated quantitative analysis of sperm morphology, reducing the subjectivity and time associated with manual annotation and providing a foundation for computer-assisted assessment of sperm quality.
